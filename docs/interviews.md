# Interviews

Owner: friend's lane. Goal: real words from real renters about repair problems. We use them in the demo, the personas, and the README. Never invent a quote.

## Before you start (read this out loud)

> Hi, we're building a tool that helps students document apartment repair problems. Can I ask you 4 quick questions about a repair problem you've had? It takes about 3 minutes. I won't write down your name. At the end I'll ask if we can quote you.

Only continue if they say yes.

## Questions

1. Have you rented off campus? What's the worst repair problem you've had?
2. How did you tell your landlord or property manager about it? Text, email, portal, phone, in person?
3. What happened next? How long did it take to get fixed, if it did?
4. Did you keep any record, like photos or messages? Did you ever need it?
5. (Optional, if time) If an app turned a photo into a written report and a draft email, would you use it? What would make you not trust it?

Tips: let them talk. Write down their exact words for the best line. Don't pitch the app until question 5.

## Consent line (ask at the end)

> Can we quote what you said in our project and demo, without your name? You can say no, or just let us use it without quoting you.

Record the answer as: Yes, quote / Use but don't quote / No.
